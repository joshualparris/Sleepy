# Sylvie's Sleepy Stories Content and Safety

## Audience

This project is for parent-guided bedtime viewing. It should support winding down, not open-ended browsing.

## Safety Commitments

- Use parent-curated links only.
- Do not expose random feeds as the primary experience.
- Keep the bedtime shelf calm and predictable.
- Avoid autoplay.
- Avoid content that is loud, frightening, ad-heavy, or designed for endless watching.

## External Video Risk

Videos open on YouTube. That means YouTube may show recommendations, comments, ads, or unrelated navigation depending on account/device settings. Parents should supervise playback and use platform-level safety controls where possible.

## Review Criteria

Before adding a video, check:

- The first 2 minutes.
- The ending.
- The thumbnail and title.
- Audio volume and pacing.
- Whether ads or sponsorships interrupt the story.
- Whether comments/recommendations are likely to distract.
- Whether the story is suitable for bedtime rather than daytime excitement.

## Recommended Metadata

Future library entries should include:

- `reviewed`: boolean.
- `reviewedBy`: parent initials or name.
- `reviewedOn`: ISO date.
- `duration`: exact or approximate duration.
- `calmness`: low, medium, high.
- `notes`: parent-only comments.
